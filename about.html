Who We Are

Clean Earth Recycled Solution Private Limited is a leading eco-friendly recycling and waste management company committed to creating a sustainable future. Since our establishment in 2025, we have been at the forefront of innovative recycling technologies and circular economy practices.

Our mission is to transform waste into valuable resources while minimizing environmental impact. We serve industries, businesses, and communities with comprehensive recycling solutions that are both efficient and environmentally responsible.

With state-of-the-art facilities and a team of dedicated professionals, we process over 10,000+ tons of waste annually, contributing significantly to reducing landfill waste and carbon emissions.

Our Mission

To pioneer zero-waste ecosystems by integrating AI-driven sorting technologies, renewable material recovery, and sustainable practices that benefit both businesses and the planet.

Our Vision

A world where every resource is endlessly reused – carbon neutral, waste free, and thriving. We envision a circular economy where nothing goes to waste.
