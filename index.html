❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our recycling services

🔹 How does your recycling process work?
We collect, sort using AI technology, process materials into raw resources, and then manufacture sustainable products — closing the loop completely.
🔹 What is the pickup availability?
We offer flexible pickup schedules: daily for industries, weekly for businesses, and on-demand for communities. Service available across Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore and expanding.
🔹 Do you handle industrial hazardous waste?
Yes! Our certified team follows CPCB guidelines for safe disposal and recycling of electronic waste, chemical containers, and industrial scrap with full documentation.
🔹 What eco-certifications do you hold?
We are ISO 14001:2015 certified, Zero Waste accredited, and recognized by the Green Business Bureau for sustainable operations.
🔹 Can individuals partner with Clean Earth?
Absolutely! Individuals can drop off recyclables at our collection centers or join our community recycling drives. Contact us for more details.
